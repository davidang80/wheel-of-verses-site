Delete your Wheel of Verses account

Wheel of Verses, by The Wheel Of Verses Team

You only have an account if you joined the leaderboard. Playing without joining creates nothing on our server.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Wheel of Verses.
  2. Tap your name or avatar at the top of the Home screen, or go to Settings → Profile & account.
  3. Tap Delete my Wheel of Verses account, then Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Delete without the app

Email support@wheelofverses.com from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Wheel of Verses account. Include your display name if you remember it. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your display name
  • All your weekly and all-time leaderboard scores
  • Your sign-in record (account ID and email address) on our server

Nothing is kept after deletion, apart from anything we are required by law to keep.

What is not affected

  • Your Google or Apple account. We never change or delete it.
  • Coins, streaks and progress saved on your phone. To clear those, use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the app.
  • A Remove Ads purchase, which belongs to your Google Play or App Store account and can be restored at any time.
