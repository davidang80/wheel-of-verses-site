Wheel of Verses

Spin the wheel, call your letters and solve the hidden Bible verse. A word game for families, church groups and Sunday school.

Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path. Psalm 119:105 (World English Bible)

Why the name? Read Why “Wheel of Verses”?

  • Wheel of Verses

    Spin, pick consonants, buy vowels and solve short verses, just like the TV game show.

  • Daily Verse

    A new verse and reflection every day, a fill-in-the-blank bonus puzzle, and a streak to keep going.

  • 4 Pictures 1 Word

    Four pictures share one Bible word. Can you find Moses, the ark or the manger?

  • 1v1 on one phone

    Pass the phone to a friend, or play against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard.

  • Works offline

    Every game works without internet. Leaderboards are optional.

  • Public-domain Bible text

    Verses come from the World English Bible.
