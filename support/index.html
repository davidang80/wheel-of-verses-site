Support

Found a bug, a typo in a verse, or have an idea? Email support@wheelofverses.com. Please include your phone model and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Common questions

Do I need internet to play?

No. Every game works offline. Internet is only used for the leaderboard, new daily verses and ads.

Which Bible translation is used?

The World English Bible (WEB), a public-domain modern English translation.

How does my streak work?

Complete the Daily Puzzle on consecutive days, using your phone's date. Missing a day starts the streak again. Changing your phone's clock backwards won't break your streak.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Go to Settings → Restore purchases while online, signed in to the same Google Play or App Store account you bought with.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my coins?

Coins and progress are stored only on the phone. Your leaderboard name and scores come back when you sign in with the same Google or Apple account.

The leaderboard says it can't connect.

Check your connection and try again later. Your solo scores are saved on your phone and sent automatically when the leaderboard is reachable.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account.
